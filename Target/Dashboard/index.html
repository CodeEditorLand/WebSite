Telemetry Architecture

Disabled Feature Gated

Feature-gated by build profile.

Telemetry and tracing are behind namedRust feature gates. The build profile determines what is compiled in - nothing is emitted unless a specific feature path is enabled.
Optional 

Opt-in OpenTelemetry. Your infrastructure only.

OpenTelemetry tracing is available in source. Activating it requires an explicit feature flag and a configured endpoint - trace data goes only to infrastructure you control.
Active 

Extension host timing. Stays in the process.

Cocoonhas internal timing and service diagnostics for extension-host work. Unless a transport is configured, that data is local process instrumentation.

Privacy & Data Rights

Exercise your GDPR rights, request data exports, or delete your account. All requests are sent directly to our privacy team and handled within 30 days.

As a resident of the EU/EEA or a user of our service, you hold the following rights. Each link opens a dedicated form page that pre-fills your account details and generates a properly formatted, income-coded request.

Request a structured copy of all personal data we hold about you under GDPR Article 15 and the Right to Data Portability (Article 20).
